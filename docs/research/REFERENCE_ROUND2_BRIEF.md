# Reference, round two: what would help next

Status: **a request, written 2026-11-17**, in answer to the owner's offer the same evening: "Want me to
also find images for certain things, and publicly attributable links you can easily reference, both for
looks and animations as well as potential physics that you could adjust in any games? I can create
several repos for it. For 2D/3D space."

Yes. The first round ([REFERENCE_IMAGES_BRIEF.md](REFERENCE_IMAGES_BRIEF.md)) is published as
[specula-imagines](https://github.com/IronAdamant/specula-imagines); the numbers behind parts 4 and 5
are in [library/](library/README.md). This is what is still missing, most wanted first. Nothing here is
needed before the work can go on: each row shortens a part when its turn comes.

## Where things go

- **Pictures** go into `specula-imagines` (more folders there), or a repository of their own when they
  are a different kind of thing (textures of real materials, for one). Unclothed plates under
  `ADULTS-ONLY/`, as now.
- **Numbers** (tables with a source, a page and a link for every value) stay in this repository, in
  [library/](library/README.md), in the form the 45 topics have: they are small, and tests are held
  against them.
- **Bulky data** (recorded motion, scans) gets a repository of its own, with its licence beside it.

The rules of the first round hold: public domain and CC0 first, then CC BY and CC BY-SA with the author
listed; anything else is a link and not a file; a licence is read from its source, never guessed; an
index row for every file.

## 3. Physics a game can adjust

Tables, each value with its source, as the library's topics are. A game's designer changes them; the
engine should start from what is real.

| Wanted | |
|---|---|
| Friction between pairs of materials (static and sliding): wood, steel, ice, rubber on dry and wet road, stone, leather, skin | coefficients |
| Bounce (restitution) for common pairs: balls of several kinds on several floors | coefficients |
| Density of common materials, and of the body's tissues | kg per cubic metre |
| Drag: coefficients of simple shapes, of a person standing and falling, of a car, of a ball | with the area each is taken on |
| Vehicles: mass, wheelbase, track, the height of the centre of mass, tyre grip, engine and brake figures for a few kinds (car, truck, motorcycle, bicycle) | tables |
| Springs and dampers of real suspensions; rolling resistance | typical ranges |
| What people can do: jump height and length, sprint and endurance speeds, reach, grip and push forces, reaction times | by sex and age where measured |
| What animals can do: mass, top speed, jump, stride for the animals of part 5 | a table |
| Thrown and shot things: speeds and masses of a thrown ball, an arrow, a sling stone | a table |
| Water: buoyancy and drag of a swimmer and of simple hulls; wave speeds | tables |
| Gravity and air on other worlds (for games not set on Earth) | a table |
