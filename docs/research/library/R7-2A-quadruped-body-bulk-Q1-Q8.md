## Sources disagree (this file)
- **Labrador chest depth.** S1's "Height of the chest (APT)" is 35.77 cm (M), but withers height minus sternum-to-ground (54.94 − 26.06) is 28.88 cm (**computed**). Either APT is measured from a different top point, or along a slope. Use APT only with that caveat; the definition is in S1 Table 1 (names only).
- **Labrador stifle angle.** S2 gives 34° ± 10° in the abstract and 31.5° (SD 15.3) in the Results (probably different side or rounding).
- **Horse thoracic and lumbar counts.** S11 gives *Equus caballus* 19 thoracic and 5 lumbar, but *E. ferus* 18 and 6; the domestic horse usually has 18 thoracic and 6 lumbar with variation (common veterinary knowledge, not re-checked here). Treat the count as varying.
- **Pig thoracic count.** S11 gives *Sus scrofa* 13 thoracic; domestic breeds are commonly reported with 14 to 17 (not re-checked here). The engine should treat this as a variable.
- **Brown bear.** S11 gives 15 thoracic and 4 lumbar for *Ursus arctos* against 14 and 6 for *U. americanus*; check before use.
