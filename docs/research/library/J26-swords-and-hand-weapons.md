Row 13 is a "Practice Sword" (a feder, 14.25.1111). Reading the table: medieval single-hand swords are 0.85 to 1.2 kg with blades 75 to 82 cm; hand-and-a-half and two-hand swords of the 15th and 16th centuries 1.2 to 1.55 kg with blades 85 to 112 cm; rapiers 1.15 to 1.5 kg with long blades of 102 to 121 cm. None of these is the 3 to 5 kg of popular myth. Two objects in the table fall outside these bands: the ca. 1400 sword 32.75.225 weighs 1,673 g with an 81.3 cm blade, the heaviest object in the table, and fits neither the 0.85-1.2 kg nor the 1.2-1.55 kg band; the 1500 Italian sword 36.149.14 has a 66 cm blade, below the 75-82 cm stated for single-hand swords. (Widened to include them, the weight band runs 0.85-1.67 kg.)

## J26-4. Swing speed, energy and timing

- Sidesword vertical cut, measured from 30 fps video with the sword's mass, length and radius of gyration measured (Vincent, Ensis Sub Caelo, "Dissecting a cut", https://blog.subcaelo.net/ensis/dissecting-cut/): peak transverse speed 13.6 m/s at 0.27 s from the start of motion; edge speed at impact about 14 m/s with about 0.5 m/s along the edge; peak total kinetic energy 28 J; transverse energy at the blade 23 J. Stopping that in 5 cm of elastic target implies about 920 N peak force (author's computation). Same post: cut duration 0.5 s, peak user power 150 W. Hobbyist measurement, one cut, rolling-shutter error acknowledged.
- Longsword cuts filmed at 100 fps (Science and Fiction, "Cutting mechanics", https://www.science-and-fiction.org/swordfighting/cuts.html): explosive cuts last 250-350 ms from first motion to impact; standard cuts with a step 600 ms or more and gain 10-20 cm of reach; angular speed at impact 1,000-2,000 °/s; the constant-speed arc lasts under 50 ms; computed energies up to about 330 J (including the arms); point-in-line cuts arrive 25-35 ms later than cuts through. Same page's comparisons: world-class javelin about 360 J, 170 lb longbow about 130 J, Roman pilum about 105 J. The same page uses a 1.2 kg sword mass and says useful fight cuts are "somewhat below 100 J". Hobbyist measurement.
- An Iaido swing-sensor figure is under Unconfirmed.

Game use: a cut is a wind-up (body), a 30-40° acceleration arc, a fast constant-speed arc (under 50 ms), then a deceleration arc; total 0.25-0.6 s.

## Sources disagree

- Longsword mass: 1.8-3 kg (Wikipedia infobox) against 1.13-1.53 kg for the Met's 15th- and 16th-century long-gripped swords (J26-1).
- Wikipedia "Longsword" against itself: the infobox gives 1.8-3 kg and a 20-30 cm grip, the prose about 2-3 kg and a 15-30 cm grip.- Cut energy: 23-28 J at the blade (sidesword, Ensis Sub Caelo) against about 330 J (longsword with arms, Science and Fiction). They measure different things: blade-only transverse energy against whole moving system.
