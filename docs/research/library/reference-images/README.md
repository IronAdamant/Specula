# Reference pictures: the lists

What [REFERENCE_IMAGES_BRIEF.md](../../REFERENCE_IMAGES_BRIEF.md) asked for, gathered by Grok at the
owner's request and delivered on 2026-11-17 in three parts ("Reference images supplied. Add them to the
library"). The owner's reason, the same day: "it's how human artists, in any profession, work. They
refer to skeleton images before putting in their touches... Even though they know what humans look
like, they still use reference and measurements."

- **A second set came the same evening** (161 pictures, for
  [REFERENCE_ROUND2_BRIEF.md](../../REFERENCE_ROUND2_BRIEF.md)): women's whole figures, two builds,
  hands and feet in more views, faces and their expressions, 57 more Muybridge plates of people,
  animals and birds, fish, insects and spiders, and a first folder for 2D.
  **[INDEX-round-2.md](INDEX-round-2.md) is its index, as the published repository has it.** 159 of
  the 161 are published. A chart of proportions by age (doll-like figures of children with no clothes
  drawn on them) and one drawing of two unclothed young women whose age nothing shows are not, and
  are kept nowhere: not their files, their rows or their links, here or there. The index has a note
  saying so, and the paths of the plates that were moved under `ADULTS-ONLY/`.
- **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md) is the first delivery's own index, kept as it came.** A row a picture: what it shows,
  the view, the subject, the page it came from with its author, the licence, the date it was checked.
  It also says how well each row of the brief is covered and which pictures were found and not taken.
- **The pictures themselves are in a public repository of their own:
  [specula-imagines](https://github.com/IronAdamant/specula-imagines)** (the owner, 2026-11-17: "a separate public repository linked to Specula
  for future references"). 269 files of the two sets, all from Wikimedia Commons, laid out as the
  indexes name them (`1-whole-body/`, `2-weak-areas/`, `3-head/`, `4-motion/`, `5-creatures/`,
  `6-2d/`), except that the 56 unclothed plates, figure studies and motion studies of adults are under
  `ADULTS-ONLY/` there, and that repository's own copies of the indexes have their paths changed to
  match. This repository links to it and never reads it.
- **The rule for what is published** (the owner, 2026-11-17, who had not looked at either delivery
  before handing it over: "I don't want to get in trouble, as the only purpose is to give AI agents
  something to reference for animation, for building in character skeletons that you can use to turn
  into real characters in the games, in different styles"): whoever places a delivery looks at every
  picture first. Unclothed adults are kept apart. **No unclothed figure of a child goes in, drawn or
  photographed, and none whose subject cannot be shown to be an adult.**

## Rights, as the index gives them

Of the first set, 88 pictures are in the public domain, 3 are CC0, and 19 are CC BY (18 under 4.0, one
under 2.0), each with its author in its row. Of the second set as delivered, 113 are in the public
domain, 28 are CC0, 11 are CC BY and 9 are CC BY-SA (a changed copy of one of those is shared under
the same licence). None is non-commercial or no-derivatives. Every licence was read from the
Commons record for the file, not guessed; three pictures whose licence was unclear or which were too
small were left as links.

The index's first paragraph says the pictures are "not to be traced, copied into a repository, or
shipped with the engine": that is the brief's own rule, written before the owner decided to publish
them. The rule that stands: **they are looked at and compared with the engine's captures, never
traced, and no picture's pixels go into an asset.**

## What is there, and what is not

Well covered: the adult male body, front, side and back, as skeleton, as muscles and as a living figure
(Albinus, Richer, Vesalius); neck and shoulders; hands; feet; knees; hips and the pelvis of each sex;
skulls and a living male head; walking and running (Muybridge), and four animals' gaits; creatures with
a skeleton each: dog, lion, horse, cow, goat, deer, bear, elephant, ostrich, bat, pterosaur, lizard,
crocodile, snake.

Thin or missing after the first set, by its index's own account: a woman's whole figure from the side,
and women in general beside the men; a whole figure at three-quarter; slender and heavy builds; a living
female head and a range of ages; elbows a little; fish, spiders and insects. The second set brought
women's whole figures, two builds, fish, insects and spiders. **Still missing:** matched front, side
and back sets of slender, muscular and older bodies; a whole figure at three-quarter; a large open
sheet of a walk cycle's key poses.

Some of the motion plates and living figures are unclothed academic studies of the 1880s and 1890s;
those are the ones kept apart. What to gather next is in
[REFERENCE_ROUND2_BRIEF.md](../../REFERENCE_ROUND2_BRIEF.md).
