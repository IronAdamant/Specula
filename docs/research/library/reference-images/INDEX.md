# Reference images for the figure

Checked: 2026-11-17.

These pictures are only for looking and comparing while the figure is brought closer to a clear, stylised human (about the level of Skyrim, not photorealism). They are not to be traced, copied into a repository, or shipped with the engine.

The brief this set was gathered from is Claude's `docs/research/REFERENCE_IMAGES_BRIEF.md`. The attached copy of that file did not land on the box or on the Mac, so this index follows the brief as transcribed in the request: sections 1 and 2 thoroughly, then 3, 4 and 5; section 6 skipped; three to six pictures per row is plenty, and a consistent set is preferred to a large one; public domain and CC0 first, then CC BY or CC BY-SA with the author listed; anything else is a link only and was not downloaded; at least 1,500 px on the long side; adults only; anatomical plates preferred to photographs.

All downloaded files come from Wikimedia Commons. Licence, author and size were taken from the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|size|extmetadata`) and were not guessed. Where a file's original was larger than 2,500 px on the long side, a 2,500 px official Commons render was kept. Four diagrams were rendered at 2,500 px wide from public-domain SVGs, which is noted in the row. The pixel size in each row is the file actually saved.

## How well each requested row is covered

- **Whole body, man, front, side, back:** well covered. Consistent sets: Albinus skeleton (front, side, back, plus an outline); Albinus écorché in the same pose; Richer surface muscles and living figure (front, side, back); Vesalius skeleton (front, side, back). Caudron adds a sculpture of one écorché in three views.
- **Whole body, woman, front, side, back:** covered for front and back (Richer 1890, p. 146-2, 169, 170, man and woman together). A standalone female side view of the whole living figure was not found at this size in an open-licence consistent set. A female skeleton, front, is included (Mitchell after Sue, with an ostrich for scale).
- **Three-quarter, whole body:** thin. Heads have a three-quarter living view (Richer p. 106). A clear whole-figure three-quarter in an open plate of the same subjects was not downloaded.
- **Skeleton and surface muscles:** well covered for the male, thinner for the female (one female skeleton; no female écorché set to match Albinus).
- **Other builds (slender, heavy, trait-varied):** thin. Richer vol. 1 p. 146-1 shows two female torsos of slightly different form. No open, high-resolution, consistent front/side/back set of slender or heavy whole bodies was found.
- **Neck and shoulders, including the collarbone:** well covered (Albinus shoulder plate, Richer neck and shoulder muscles, living upper body).
- **Hands, including the wrist:** well covered (Albinus, Vesalius, Richer bones and muscles, palm).
- **Feet, including the ankle and toes:** well covered (Albinus, Vesalius, Richer bones and muscles).
- **Knees:** well covered (Richer living front and side, Richer bones, Gray tibial plateau).
- **Elbows:** covered (Richer bones and a flexed living elbow). A little thinner than the knee set.
- **Hips, including how they meet the trunk, and male/female pelvis:** well covered (Richer pelvis, hip side, female hips front, and a male/female pelvis comparison).
- **Heads, for the faces' lane:** well covered for skull (front, side and further views) and for a living male head (front, side, three-quarter). Thinner for a living female head as a dedicated plate, and for a wide range of ages.
- **Motion, human walk and run:** well covered (Muybridge). A walking man in a pelvis cloth and a draped plate were preferred where they existed; unclothed scientific walk and run plates are also included because they show the joints clearly, and are academic motion studies, not portraits.
- **Motion, animals:** covered for horse walk and gallop, dog gallop, cat gallop, and an elk/moose walk.
- **Creatures by body plan, each with a skeleton diagram:**
  - Digitigrade mammal (dog, lion): well covered, skeleton plus other views.
  - Unguligrade (horse, cow, goat, deer): well covered. Horse has a de Sève skeleton plus Richer 1910 plates and Ellenberger hind views; cow is a consistent Ellenberger set (skeleton, surface, muscles, front, rear).
  - Plantigrade mammal (bear): covered (de Blainville, skeleton and head).
  - Proboscidean (elephant): covered as a comparison with the human skeleton (Hawkins, 1860).
  - Bird (ostrich): covered (photograph of a whole skeleton, side).
  - Bat / winged four-limbed body: covered (Buvée bat skeleton, plus wing and leg; Williston pterosaur).
  - Sprawling reptile (lizard, crocodile): covered (Williston).
  - Snake: covered (Williston).
  - **Fish: missing.** No open-licence plate at 1,500 px or more was found in this pass.
  - **Spider: missing.** Same reason.
  - **Insect: not downloaded.** See the link-only row below.
  - **Domestic cat skeleton: not downloaded.** A Gallica plate exists but is only about 520 px; the lion plates stand in for a felid body plan.
- **Section 6:** skipped, as asked.
