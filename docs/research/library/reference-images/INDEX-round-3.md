# Reference images, round three

Checked: 2026-11-17.

These pictures are only for looking and comparing. They are not to be traced, copied into a repository, or shipped with the engine. Nothing here was pushed anywhere.

> **Note added when this set was published (2026-11-17).** The sentence above is the brief the set was
> gathered under. Specula's owner had the pictures published in this repository, which every picture's
> licence allows. What stands: they are looked at and compared, never traced, and no picture's pixels go
> into an asset or ship with the engine. Changed from the delivery: the folders are numbered on from
> the earlier sets' (`7-fire-smoke` to `13-weapons-fencing`, where the delivery had `1-` to `7-`), and
> the paths below say so. **Three photographs of the delivery are not here** (88 of its 91 are), because
> they show mostly people at a disaster or at work and little of the subject; their rows are taken out
> of the table below, and these are their pages:
> - `fire-in-the-hole-130219-f-zc102-046.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fire_in_the_hole_130219-F-ZC102-046.jpg
> - `2023-turkey-earthquake-damage.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2023_Turkey_Earthquake_Damage.jpg
> - `izmir-earthquake-aftermath.jpg`: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Izmir_earthquake_aftermath.jpg

Rules followed: public domain and CC0 first, then CC BY and CC BY-SA with the author named; anything else is a link only and was not downloaded; licences were read from the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=url|size|extmetadata`), never guessed. No unclothed figures of anyone were needed or included this round, so there is no ADULTS-ONLY folder. Disaster photos were chosen to show structures, not victims.

Folders are new for this round: `1-fire-smoke`, `2-lightning`, `3-explosion`, `4-light-dark`, `5-shape-bounce`, `6-destruction`, `7-weapons-fencing` (fencing treatise plates and Met CC0 weapon photos, added for the brief's section 3).

Size rule: as in round two, each file is the largest standard Commons render whose long side is at most 2,500 px, or the original when smaller. The pixel size in each row is the file actually saved.

Separators: in the source and licence cells, `·` separates the link from the author or licence name.
