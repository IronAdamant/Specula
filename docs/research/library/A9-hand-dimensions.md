# A9 — Hand dimensions: Greiner 1991 joined to ANSUR II

Units cm unless marked. Accessed 2026-11-19.

**Main source.** Greiner, T.M. (1991), *Hand Anthropometry of U.S. Army Personnel*, Technical Report NATICK/TR-92/011, US Army Natick RD&E Center; DTIC ADA244533. Scan: https://archive.org/details/DTIC_ADA244533 (Approved for public release; distribution unlimited; US government work).
**Who was measured.** A subsample of the 1987–88 US Army anthropometric survey (ANSUR): **women n = 1,304, men n = 1,003**, active-duty soldiers (adults). Values below were read from the per-dimension statistics pages (one page per dimension; women in the left-hand block, men in the right). Report page = 41 + 2 × dimension number (IA scan leaf = 54 + 2 × dimension number).
**Definitions that matter.** *Link* values are joint-centre-to-joint-centre approximations from photographed landmarks, not bone lengths. *Height* = perpendicular distance from the fingertip to the wrist-crease baseline. *Crotch height* = depth of the web between digits measured from the wrist-crease baseline. D1 = thumb, D2 = index … D5 = little finger.

**How to use the join (computed by me, a method note, not a measured value):** to scale any Greiner finger dimension to an ANSUR II hand, multiply by (ANSUR II hand length ÷ Greiner measured hand length) for lengths and (ANSUR II hand breadth ÷ Greiner measured hand breadth) for breadths/circumferences. The factors are ~1.00 for length and 0.98 for breadth, so the 1987–88 finger detail fits the 2012 population almost as printed.

## Sources disagree

- **Palm length:** Greiner dim 61 (women 10.09, men 11.05) vs ANSUR II palm length (10.87, 11.65). The gap (0.6–0.8 cm) is far larger than for hand length or breadth, so the two reports almost certainly use different end landmarks; I did not confirm the exact ANSUR II definition in this round. Do not join these two directly.
- **Dims 19 and 31** (D2 distal link, D3 distal link) print identical statistics (2.55/0.21 and 2.84/0.23). Possibly a duplicated page in the report. D2 phalanx links also sum to more than the printed D2 link (women +0.29 cm, men +0.35 cm), while D3–D5 sum to within ±0.03 cm. If D2 distal is solved from the printed D2 link, it would be women 2.26, men 2.49 (computed by me), which fits the index finger better than 2.55/2.84.
- **Dim 58 equals dim 23** for both sexes (digitizer hand length = D3 height), which is expected by definition; for men dim 59 also equals dim 58 (19.41), while for women dim 59 (18.07) is 0.28 cm longer.
- **D1 proximal phalanx link (1.92 / 2.11) is shorter than D1 distal link (3.08 / 3.45).** Anatomically the thumb's proximal phalanx is the longer bone; the inversion comes from Greiner's landmark choice (the distal link runs to the fingertip). Treat thumb links as landmark spacings, not bone lengths.

## Not found

- Hand thickness at the knuckles (metacarpal depth) and palm thickness: not measured by Greiner 1991; ANSUR II has no hand-thickness variable either. Not found in an open US source.
- Fingertip pad and nail dimensions by sex in a large sample: not found in Greiner. Small open sample: Boike et al. 2025 (CC BY, see A11) gives thumb/index nail widths.
- Hands by build or age group: Greiner gives no build/age breakdown; not found.
