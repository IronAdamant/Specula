## Sources disagree (this file)
- **Giant-pterosaur flight limit:** 41 kg and 5.1 m (Sato et al. 2009) and 70 kg (Chatterjee & Templin 2004) vs 200-250 kg flying with a quadrupedal launch (D6, D7). D6 argues that bird-based limits do not apply to pterosaurs. Henderson's 544 kg model, which D6 corrects to about 240 kg, sits in between.
- **Launch force depends on the method:** the ptarmigan figures (D5) are from one leg on the force plate, divided by the whole bird's weight (22.91 N ÷ (0.5515 kg × 9.81) ≈ 4.2, **computed**), so whole-body force would be higher. The starling, quail and vampire bat figures (D3, D4) come from abstracts that do not say whether one limb or the whole body was measured.
- **D1 internal errors:** (a) *Artibeus lituratus* LD3 is printed as 68.38 ± 6.58 mm, the same as its forearm (68.38 ± 2.92) and shorter than its LD4 (105.40). This looks like a typo; do not use that LD3. (b) D1's text gives the smallest LD3 (50.31 mm) to "*Eptesicus fuscus*", but Table 1 gives 50.31 mm to *Eptesicus furinalis*, and *E. fuscus* is not in the table.
