## Sources disagree

1. **Fruit fly top step rate:** Mendes 2013 gives a plateau of ~16 cycles/s (≈ 60 ms period), while Ramdya 2017's model walks at 20 Hz. The 20 Hz is a model setting, not a measurement.
2. **Stick insect column order:** the search-index synthesis for Theunissen 2015 labelled the first coxa/femur/tibia triplet "front leg". The table header runs hind → middle → front, so that triplet is the hind leg. Grok read it the same way as me. Confirmed by Library Verify 2026-11-17: the header runs hind, middle, front, and rows 1.1–1.3 are mapped accordingly (N = 10 per species for mass).
3. **Ant duty-factor crossing:** Wahl 2015 says the duty factor falls below 0.5 above ~370 mm/s overall, but the per-leg crossings are 132 (hind), 182 (front) and 369 (middle). Use the middle-leg value as the run threshold.
4. **Hawkmoth amplitude:** Zheng 2013 (one moth, hovering) gives 100.3°. Grok's reading of Willmott & Ellington 1997 gives ~115–120° for hover. The two are not reconciled.
5. **Spider leg 2 coupling:** in Weihmann 2013, the left–right phase of leg 2 is uniform (uncoupled, R = 0.09), while legs 1, 3 and 4 are near 0.5. A clean "two alternating tetrapods" animation is a simplification.
