## Sources disagree

- Kashiyama 2021: Methods says 334 women; Table 1 counts sum to 335.
- Temporal hairline: Kashiyama (Japanese) four types roughly equal in women (20–28% each) vs Nusbaum (White women)
  concave triangle 61% — population or classification difference.
- Loussouarn 2016: text says diameters range 69 µm (French) to 89 µm (Chinese), but Table 2 lists South-African 68 µm;
  Brazilian density SD printed as "772" (likely 72).
- Whorl CCW share: 8.4% (Klar 2003, US public) vs 29.5% (Luo 2023, Chinese single-whorl adults).
- Rajput 2021: 70–120 grafts (abstract) vs 150–200 (text, thick male brow).
- Grunwald 2025: "≈300 vellus hairs per 0.5 mm² of each nostril (area 8 × 6 mm)" — the stated area (48 mm²) and 0.5 mm²
  do not match; Table 1 caption also wrongly says it describes face-touch frequency.
- Tomb Raider slide shows "1.752.3" for sort/apply/fog in close-up (garbled; total 7.5559 ms).

- **Widow's peak prevalence (surfaced by Grok, confirmed):** 29.6% W / 32.8% M (Kashiyama, Japanese) vs 81% (Nusbaum, US salon women) vs 94.17% (Ceballos, Spanish women). The scoring rules and ages differ. Do not pool these.
- **Mid-eyebrow to frontal midpoint:** 5.5 cm (Nusbaum) vs 5.89 cm (Ceballos, as compared by Grok; I confirmed only Nusbaum's 5.5 cm). Kashiyama measures glabella to hairline (6.2 cm W), which is a different landmark.
- **Luo 2023 CCW share (reconciled):** the pooled figure 1,208/4,099 = 29.5% (Open Targets / GWAS Catalog) holds. Grok's Supplementary Table S1 split — NSPT 1,497 CW / 652 CCW (30.3%) and TZL 1,394 CW / 556 CCW (28.5%) — sums exactly to those totals (652+556 = 1,208; 1,497+652 = 2,149; 1,394+556 = 1,950; 2,149+1,950 = 4,099). There is no conflict. The per-cohort split itself is **UNVERIFIABLE** here (S1 not opened; JID PDF 403).
- **Luo 2023 licence:** Crossref's version-of-record licence is CC BY 4.0 (`http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/`; also “cc by” on Europe PMC). The University of Edinburgh Research Explorer labels its copy of the published PDF “CC BY-NC-ND” (https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/gwass-identify-genetic-loci-associated-with-human-scalp-hair-whor/ , accessed 2026-11-17). **Treat as CC BY-NC-ND until resolved.** Numbers are cited as facts either way.
- **Klar:** 8.4% CCW (Klar 2003, US) vs "random" orientation (Klar 2009, Japanese). The same author treats this as real population variation.
- **Bhokare 2024 hardware:** the paper prints "GTX 4090". No GTX-branded 4090 exists (NVIDIA's 4090 is RTX-branded), so this is probably a typo in the paper. Grok repeated it.
- **Kashiyama 2021 Fig. 2 legend:** panel A is labelled the lateral horizontal line in the legend, but the text cites Fig. 2A for the mid-frontal line.
