# A14 — Muscle thickness (ultrasound / MRI)

Accessed 2026-11-19. Thin file: open tables of muscle thickness by site and sex are scarce; most large studies print them as figures or behind paywalls.

## 1. Medial gastrocnemius thickness by age and sex (ultrasound)

**Source.** Sakakibara, Saito, Nakamura, Imura et al. (2026), "Investigation of age- and sex-specific reference values for lower-limb muscle thickness using ultrasonography", *J Rural Med* 21:451, doi:10.2185/jrm.2026-047, Table 2, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13643393/ (article CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; numbers quoted only).
**Who.** 666 Japanese adults aged 18+ (296 men, mean age 41.8; 370 women, mean age 54.6), health-checkup users in Akita and visitors to a health centre in Aichi, 2025–26. Prone, knee straight, ankle relaxed; 10 MHz probe at 30% of the popliteal-fossa-to-lateral-malleolus distance; the paper defines thickness as the "distance from the skin surface to the deep fascia" (so it **includes** the fat layer; see Sources disagree).

| Age | Men n | Men MT cm | Women n | Women MT cm |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20–29 | 76 | 2.03 ± 0.35 | 22 | 1.87 ± 0.31 |
| 30–39 | 114 | 2.02 ± 0.36 | 35 | 1.98 ± 0.35 |
| 40–49 | 20 | 2.17 ± 0.32 | 53 | 1.80 ± 0.30 |
| 50–59 | 32 | 1.98 ± 0.31 | 131 | 1.80 ± 0.26 |
| 60–69 | 18 | 1.80 ± 0.37 | 66 | "17.3" ± 0.25 (printed; almost certainly 1.73) |
| 70–79 | 26 | 1.63 ± 0.22 | 49 | 1.63 ± 0.30 |
| ≥ 80 | 10 | 1.62 ± 0.28 | 14 | 1.46 ± 0.22 |

Correlation of thickness with BMI: men r = 0.495, women r = 0.405 (Table 3).

## 2. Quadriceps thickness relative to thigh length, by age group (ultrasound)

**Source.** Abe T., Sakamaki M., Yasuda T., Bemben M.G., Kondo M., Kawakami Y., Fukunaga T. (2011), "Age-related, site-specific muscle loss in 1507 Japanese men and women aged 20 to 95 years", *J Sports Sci Med* 10:145–150, https://www.jssm.org/jssm-10-145.xml%3EFulltext (Results text; per-site tables are printed as figures). **Who:** 722 men and 785 women aged 20–95, Japan (Table 1: young men 1.70 m / 66.8 kg; young women 1.58 m / 52.0 kg). B-mode, 5 MHz, standing; thickness = fat–muscle interface to bone.

Quadriceps thickness ÷ thigh length, cm per m, mean (SD): men 20–39: 1.35 (0.19); 40–59: 1.23 (0.19); 60–95: 1.03 (0.20). Women 20–39: 1.25 (0.18); 40–59: 1.18 (0.20); 60–95: 1.00 (0.19).

Eight sites were measured (biceps, triceps, abdomen, subscapula, quadriceps, hamstrings, tibialis anterior, triceps surae), but the absolute values per site are only in figures (Fig. 1–2 of the article): **not copied**.

## Sources disagree

- **What "thickness" means:** Sakakibara 2026 measures skin to deep fascia (fat included, by its wording), Abe 2011 measures fat–muscle interface to bone (muscle only). Not comparable directly.
- **Sakakibara Table 2, women 60–69:** printed "17.3", which is ten times its neighbours; read as a typo for 1.73 (not corrected in the table above; flagged).
