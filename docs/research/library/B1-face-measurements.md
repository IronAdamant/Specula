# B1 — Head and face measurements (adults, by sex, with spreads)

> Verify fixes applied 2026-11-16: bizygomatic men n=2542, IPD women n=1452 (Table VI); NIOSH licence wording corrected in 'How to use'.

Prepared 2026-11-16 for the Specula library. All links accessed 2026-11-16. Every number below was read
directly from the source PDF or supplementary table named in its row (see Verification note). Units are mm
unless a table says cm. Measurement names follow each source's own definitions; where two sources measure
"the same" thing by different landmarks or instruments, they are kept in separate tables, not merged.

## How to use these tables

- **Primary tables (large n):** ANSUR II (Table 1, US government work, public domain) and NIOSH 2003 survey (Table 2, figures cited as facts from Zhuang & Bradtmiller 2005, JOEH, © Taylor & Francis).
  These cover the head box (length, breadth, height, circumference), face length and width, jaw width,
  interpupillary distance, nose width and length, mouth width and ears.
- **Feature table (eyes, nose, lips, face heights):** 3D Facial Norms adults 22–25 (Table 3), the only
  open-licence source here for intercanthal width, eye fissure length, lip heights and philtrum.
- **Face thirds with forehead (trichion):** Turkish adults (Table 4); no US large-n source measured trichion.
- Ratios for face "fifths" and thirds (Table 5) are **computed by me** from Table 3 means, not quoted.

the intercanthal gap, the face is about 4.7–4.8 eye-widths wide (not 5), and the lower third is ~1.2–1.3×
the nose height. These are means; individual spread is large (see SDs).

## Sources disagree

- **Head length / breadth, ANSUR vs NIOSH:** ANSUR men 199.5 × 154.3 mm vs NIOSH men 197.3 × 153.0 mm;
  ANSUR women 189.8 × 147.8 vs NIOSH 187.5 × 146.8. Differences are 1–2 mm, within measurer/population
  variation (soldiers vs workers; ANSUR women's values include some braids).
- **Interpupillary distance:** ANSUR 64.0 (M) / 61.7 (F) mm vs NIOSH 64.5 / 61.9 mm — close agreement.
- **Face width (zy–zy):** ANSUR 142.6 (M) / 133.8 (F); NIOSH 143.5 / 135.1; 3DFN adults 136.7 / 129.2;
  Turkish 129.1 / 127.2. The 3DFN and Turkish values are younger/leaner or different populations and much
  narrower; caliper pressure on soft tissue also differs.
- **Mouth width:** NIOSH 51.1 / 48.0; 3DFN 50.6 / 48.0; Turkish 51.6 / 48.9 — good agreement.
- **Nose width:** NIOSH 36.6 / 33.2; 3DFN 36.0 / 32.4; Turkish 35.2 / 32.3.
- **Intercanthal width:** 3DFN 33.0 / 31.3 (3D, US whites); Turkish 33.2 / 31.9 (caliper).
- **Eye fissure width:** 3DFN 28.6 / 27.7 (3D, en–ex) vs Turkish 33.9 / 33.4 (caliper). A ~5 mm
  disagreement — likely landmark placement (3D software vs caliper at canthi); flag before use.
- **Face length (menton–sellion):** ANSUR 122.6 (M) vs NIOSH 122.7; women 113.1 vs 113.4 — agree.
- **Ear length:** ANSUR 64.2 (M) / 59.4 (F) vs Turkish sa–sba 61.5 / 58.8.
