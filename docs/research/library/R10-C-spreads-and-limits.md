The two studies use different reference frames and signs (seated Optotrak versus standing CT), so their numbers are not interchangeable.

## C4. Joint centres from landmarks, women, more than 8 people

**Hara et al. 2016 (C-S7).** CT of 157 deceased people (84 male, 73 female). Groups of 30 per sex for adolescents (16-19) and adults (25-40), plus 24 boys and 13 girls aged 5-11. Hip joint centre (HJC) located visually in 3D Slicer; accuracy 1.1 mm (SD 0.3). Origin is the midpoint between left and right ASIS; X posterior-anterior (negative is posterior), Y medial-lateral, Z inferior-superior. Supplementary Table 1, mean (SD), mm:

| Group | n | HJC X | HJC Y | HJC Z | Inter-ASIS | Leg length |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All women | 73 | -43 (8) | 81 (10) | -74 (9) | 210 (25) | 816 (79) |
| Women 16-19 | 30 | -46 (6) | 83 (4) | -78 (5) | 211 (18) | 847 (37) |
| Women 25-40 | 30 | -45 (6) | 87 (6) | -76 (6) | 224 (18) | 845 (46) |
| Men 25-40 | 30 | -45 (7) | 87 (5) | -81 (8) | 230 (16) | 910 (61) |

Recommended equations (sex and age did not need separate equations; main text p.3), LL = leg length in mm:

- HJC X = 11 - 0.063 LL
- HJC Y = 8 + 0.086 LL
- HJC Z = -9 - 0.078 LL

Leave-one-out prediction error with leg length as predictor: 5.2, 4.4 and 3.8 mm (posterior-anterior, medial-lateral, inferior-superior).

Worked check (mine): a woman with LL 845 mm gives X = 11 - 53.2 = -42 mm, Y = 8 + 72.7 = 81 mm, Z = -9 - 65.9 = -75 mm, close to the adult women's means.

Knee and ankle centres in women from landmarks with SDs: not found in an open source this round. Gap.

Reading (mine): in ANSUR II the observed extremes sit about 3 to 5 SD from the mean for lengths (stature women: (140.90 - 162.85) / 6.42 = -3.4 SD, (182.90 - 162.85) / 6.42 = +3.1 SD). Weight is right-skewed (skewness about 0.5) and its maximum is about 4.7 SD above the female mean ((119.60 - 67.76) / 10.98). Army entry standards cut both tails, so these are not general-population limits. For a generator this suggests: clamp lengths at about plus or minus 3.5 SD for "ordinary adults", and treat anything beyond as a deliberate "extreme" mode bounded by the Guinness values.
