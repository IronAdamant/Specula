**Left hind at 0 (as the brief asks):** F1 gives no fore-to-hind phase, so the full four-foot phase set with LH = 0 cannot be built from F1. The review F2 gives, for the walk, "a lateral advanced placement (or limb phasing value) close to 0.25", with footfalls in the order LH, LF, RH, RF, and for the trot a limb phasing of 0.50 with LH + RF together and RH + LF together. On that basis the phases relative to LH = 0 would be: **walk LH 0, LF 0.25, RH 0.50, RF 0.75; trot LH 0 and RF 0, RH 0.50 and LF 0.50** (**computed** from F2's limb phasing value and F1's left-right ratio of 0.50; this is an idealised pattern, not a measured mean with spread).

**Duty factors from the review F2 (compiled, no n):** walk 0.70 to 0.60 (slower to faster), trot 0.55 to 0.30, canter about 0.30, fast gallop about 0.20 (all hind-limb support as a share of the stride). Speeds (F2): walk about 1.4-1.8 m/s; trot about 2.5-6.5 m/s (8.5-12.0 in harness racing); canter/gallop about 9.0-15.0 m/s, 17.0-19.0 in racing. F2's gallop sequence: transverse, LH, RH, LF, RF in a right-lead gallop; canter: LH, then RH + LF, then RF (right lead).

## Not found (this file)
- Horse phases with LH = 0 as measured means with spread for walk, trot, canter and gallop (F1 uses LF = 0 and reports only the left-right ratios).
- Domestic cat: per-limb phases and duty factors for walk, trot and gallop with spread, and the walk-trot and trot-gallop transition speeds from an opened primary. Leads not opened: Halbertsma 1983 (*Acta Physiol Scand Suppl* 521, PubMed 6582764; the search extract says cats "slower than approximately 3 m/s" alternate the limbs); a 2026 *J Neurophysiol* paper (doi:10.1152/jn.00240.2026; the search extract reports walking duty factors of 68.23% ± 3.43 on a treadmill and 61.15% ± 2.92 over ground at 0.5-0.8 m/s for 10 cats). The page timed out, so these are **not verified** and are not used in the tables.
- Bound and half-bound phases and duty factors for any species: not found in an open source (Hildebrand 1977 and the Wetzel series are paywalled).

## Sources disagree (this file)
- Horse duty factor at canter: F1 (Icelandic horses, 6-7 m/s) gives about 0.32-0.35 (**computed**), close to F2's "0.30 in the canter". In the trot, F1's 0.36-0.43 sits inside F2's 0.55-0.30 range. F1's walk value of about 0.60 at 1.5-2 m/s is at the fast end of F2's 0.70-0.60.
- Speed ranges: F1's Icelandic horses cantered and galloped at 4-9 m/s. F2 puts the canter/gallop at about 9-15 m/s for horses in general. Breed and rider practice differ.
