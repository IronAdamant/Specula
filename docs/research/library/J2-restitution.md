# J2: Bounce (coefficient of restitution)

Researched 2026-11-17 (all URLs accessed 2026-11-17). Sports governing bodies publish drop-and-rebound tests for their balls. I computed a coefficient of restitution from each.

**How e was computed (by me):** e = √(h_rebound / h_drop). This ignores air drag, which matters for a light, fuzzy tennis ball, so the true e at impact is a little higher than shown. It also assumes both heights are measured to the same point on the ball. FIBA says it measures both to the underside of the ball; ITF and ITTF do not say in the text I read.

## Sources disagree

- Table tennis rebound: the ITTF leaflet (via mirror) gives 240–260 mm from 305 mm; Nittaku's product notes give 240–265 mm (e up to 0.932, computed). Use the ITTF range.

## Not found

- Restitution for everyday material pairs (wood on wood, steel on concrete, rubber on wood) in an open primary table. Not added rather than guessed.
- Golf and baseball restitution limits: not read from the rule documents in this pass.
