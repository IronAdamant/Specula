# J27: Sound: loudness at set distances and fall-off

Researched 2026-11-17 (all URLs accessed 2026-11-17). Brief section 3, "Sound". Open sound libraries and licences are in links.md. Thunder timing is in J13; blast overpressure in J14.

## J27-1. Fall-off with distance (computed by me)

For a small source in open air, sound pressure falls as 1/r, so level falls 20 log10(r₂/r₁) dB: −6.0 dB per doubling of distance; −12.0 dB at 4×, −18.1 at 8×, −24.1 at 16×, −30.1 at 32×, −36.1 at 64×, −42.1 at 128×. Air absorption adds more loss at high frequencies over long distances (ISO 9613-1; not opened this session). A line source such as a busy road falls about 3 dB per doubling (standard result; not sourced here).
