## Sources disagree (this file)
- **Femoral adduction in the high walk:** C1 cites about 55° (Gatesy 1991), while C1's own five alligators at 0.146 m/s ranged from 19° to 55° at mid-stance, so most of its strides were less upright than "55°". The two are not strictly comparable: C1 measures relative to the animal's body, and the 55° figure is "below horizontal".
- **Size range vs proportions:** in *Varanus* the hind limb / SVL rises with size (about 0.29 in *V. brevicauda* to 0.39-0.40 in *V. gouldii*, *V. giganteus* and *V. komodoensis*; **computed**), whereas the small geckos in H1 have a hind limb / SVL of about 0.43-0.49 (**computed**). Methods differ (V1: preserved specimens to the nearest 0.5 mm; H1: live and museum animals with calipers), so cross-paper comparisons carry method error.
- **Tortoise tail:** males have a longer tail than females in both Serbian populations (TL/SCL about 0.29-0.30 vs 0.15-0.17, **computed**), so a single tail ratio for *Testudo* would be misleading.
