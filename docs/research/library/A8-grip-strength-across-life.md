## A8-1. Normative grip strength, Great Britain

Source: Dodds R.M. et al. (2014), "Grip strength across the life course: normative data from twelve British studies", *PLoS ONE* 9(12): e113637, doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0113637, licence CC BY (Europe PMC record). Abstract read 2026-11-17 via https://europepmc.org/article/PMC/PMC4256164 (or https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0113637); centile curves are in the paper's figures and tables (not retyped).

What was measured: hand-held dynamometer grip (maximum of trials), 60,803 observations from 49,964 people (26,687 female) in 12 general-population studies, ages 4 to 90.

| Quantity | Males | Females |
|---|---|---|
| Peak median grip | 51 kg | 31 kg |
| Age range of the peak | 29-39 years | 26-42 years |
| "Weak grip" (at least 2.5 SD below the sex-specific peak mean) prevalence at age 80 | 23% | 27% |

Shape of the life curve (same source): rise to a peak in early adult life, plateau through to midlife, decline from midlife onwards; males stronger than females from adolescence.

Converted (computed by me, g = 9.81 m/s²): 51 kg-force = 500 N; 31 kg-force = 304 N.
