Prepared 2026-11-16. All links accessed 2026-11-16. The FACS manual (Ekman, Friesen & Hager 2002) is a
copyrighted, paid product and was **not** used or copied. AU numbers, short names and the muscle behind each
are taken from an open-access CC BY review that tabulates them (Clark et al. 2020); everything else is
described in my own words. No figures or wording from any manual are reproduced.

## Summary

- 30 commonly used AUs and their muscles are listed in Table 1 (CC BY source).
- Basic-expression AU recipes: the FACS Investigator's Guide rules (via Clark et al. 2020, Table 3) and the
  stricter CK+ criteria (Lucey et al. 2010, Table 2) are in Table 2; 17 measured compound expressions
  (Du & Martinez 2015) are in Table 3.
- Measured movement sizes in mm (Table 4): a closed smile moves the mouth corner about 9–10 mm in adults; a
  pout moves it about 17–18 mm; a maximal brow raise lifts the mid-brow about 7.57–9.20 mm.
- Timing (Table 5): smile onset (neutral to peak) ~0.54–0.60 s; brow-raise onset ~0.49 s (posed) to ~0.74 s
  (spontaneous); micro-expressions last under ~0.5 s with onsets under ~0.26 s.

## Sources disagree

- **Anger recipe:** CK+ requires AU23+AU24 (Table 2b), while the FACS Investigator's Guide rules (via Clark)
  accept anger variants without AU23/24 (e.g. 4+5/7), and the unconfirmed Du 2014 prototype is 4+7+24.
- **Sadness:** FACS rules accept AU1 alone or 1+4; CK+ needs 1+4+15 or 11 (with 6+15 exception).
- **Surprise:** CK+ caps AU5 at intensity B; FACS rules allow 1+2+5+26/27 without that cap; Du 2014
  (unconfirmed) uses 1+2+25+26 without AU5.
- **Brow-raise size:** 7.57–9.20 mm central excursion (da Cruz 2004, Table 1, 2D photos, Brazil) vs 5.6 mm average
  "width of eyebrow displacement" (Kwon 2023, 3D). Different metrics (vertical excursion vs a
  displacement extent), so not directly comparable.
- **Clark Table 1 typos:** AU10 is printed as "Upper lid raiser" (should be lip) and "Masetter"; corrected
  in my table as noted.
- **CK+ figure caption** (PDF p. 3) labels an example "Disgust – AU 1+4+15+17", which contradicts its own
  Table 2 rule (AU9 or AU10). Treat the caption as an error; use Table 2.
