## Sources disagree

- A4 Table 1's caption says the bracketed numbers are 25% and 75% percentiles, but they look like confidence limits around a regression coefficient (for example a = 33.47 (32.01, 34.93)). I copied only the central values.
- Duty factor at low speed: tarantulas at 0.02 to 0.11 m/s give DF 0.58 to 0.71 (A1, A2); the *Cupiennius* fit gives 0.57 to 0.68 at 0.1 m/s (computed). Broadly consistent; stride frequency differs a lot (0.6 to 0.7 s⁻¹ in *E. weijenberghi* vs about 2.2 to 2.7 Hz computed for *Cupiennius* at 0.1 m/s), as expected from the speed difference.
