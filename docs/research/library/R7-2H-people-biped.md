## Sources disagree

- Inferior angle of the scapula: textbooks commonly say T7 (as H4 reports); the pooled data (H4) and radiographs (H5) put it at the T8 spinous process / upper T9 body on average.
- H10 Table 3 reports stance time 0.15 s alongside a stance share of 63% at 107 steps/min, which do not agree (a step at 107 steps/min lasts about 0.56 s, **computed**); the t-test statistics in Tables 3 and 4 also look like those of a much smaller sample than 20. Use H10's angles with caution.
- ANSUR II waist (at the navel, 980 mm) sits slightly **below** iliocristale height (996 mm) on average in women, while many garment standards place the "natural waist" above the iliac crest. This is a definition difference, not an error.
