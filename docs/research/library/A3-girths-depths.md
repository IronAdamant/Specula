The pattern to build from: girths and depths follow **mass** (r 0.7–0.93) and barely follow stature (r 0.2–0.4). At a fixed mass, a taller person has a slightly *smaller* girth (negative b2), meaning slimmer for the same weight. Wrist, ankle and shoulder circumference are the exceptions: they track frame (stature) more.

## Sources disagree

- **Military vs civilian**: NHANES men 20–39 have mean waist 98.2 cm (SD 17.2) vs ANSUR II men 94.1 cm (SD 11.2), and mean weight 90.3 kg vs 85.5 kg. NHANES spread is about 1.5× larger. **ANSUR understates heavy builds**, as the OpenLab page itself warns.
- **Waist landmark**: ANSUR waist is measured at the omphalion (navel). NHANES waist uses its own landmark, defined in its procedures manual (not opened, so not confirmed here). The values are not strictly comparable.
- **Hip**: ANSUR "buttock circumference" (max buttock) vs NHANES "hip circumference". The names and landmarks differ.
