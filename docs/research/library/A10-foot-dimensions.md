## Sources disagree

- **Lateral malleolus height:** Parham (1985 recruits) men 7.26 / women 6.59 vs ANSUR II men 7.29 / women 6.27. Men agree; women differ by 0.32 cm. Different eras and samples; landmark (tip vs most lateral point) not compared in this round.
- **Ankle circumference:** Parham men 22.38 / women 20.82 vs ANSUR II 22.93 / 21.57 — ANSUR II about 0.6–0.75 cm larger. Possible causes: a heavier 2012 population; definitions not compared.
- **Foot length:** Parham right foot men 26.96, women 24.38 vs ANSUR II 27.12, 24.63 (close).
- **Parham prints values to two decimals that I read visually from scanned pages** (no OCR on the box). The checker re-read every value (see CHECKER-REPORT.md).

## Not found

- **Footprint (arch index) values for low, middle and high arches by sex** in an open source: not found this round. Images of high-arch footprints are in images/4-feet (CC0); Munson 1912 footprint figures (PD) show the range.
- **Foot loaded vs unloaded (sitting vs standing) length and breadth change:** not found in an open source. Related only: Knapik et al. 1990, USARIEM T1-91, DTIC ADA230384 (15 male soldiers; foot measures before and after 12 km loaded treadmill marches, with significant pre-to-post changes in ball-of-foot measures); this is swelling after a march, not loaded vs unloaded standing.
