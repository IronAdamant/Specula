## 2. Lessons that repeat across makers (my synthesis of the rows above, not a source claim)

- **Determinism and local stability are stated design goals**, not afterthoughts: HZD (P7, "Deterministic", "Locally stable"), Far Cry 5 (P8, same result on any build machine), Wildlands (P9, non-deterministic tiles caused seams), No Man's Sky (P4, a point must be the same for every player). [P7, P8, P9, P4 URLs above · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Rules beat painted results once the terrain keeps changing:** FC5 hand-painted forests went incoherent; HZD and FC5 both regenerate placement from rules plus painted masks. [https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2018/presentations/ProceduralWorldGeneration.pdf · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Gameplay caps density and variety:** FC5 forests too dense for AI; Starfield "too game-y" with too many finds; Rain World too free or too restricted. [P8, P14, P17 URLs · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Perceived sameness is the main quality risk**, not mathematical uniqueness (Compton's oatmeal, P3). [https://web.archive.org/web/20240109201851/https://galaxykate0.tumblr.com/post/139774965871/so-you-want-to-build-a-generator · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Artist control is the selling point to the team:** "Nothing is random" (Wildlands), "Balance between control and automation" (FC5), ecotopes made by one person (HZD). [P9, P8, P7 URLs · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Procedural animation of unknown bodies works for broad motions but fails on contact** (self-collision, "hug", "rub your chin") and needs animator intuition built over weeks (Spore, P1). [https://doi.org/10.1145/1360612.1360626 · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Precision:** planet-scale worlds break float precision (NMS P5, Elite Dangerous P13). [P5, P13 URLs · accessed 2026-11-17]

## Sources disagree

- **Horizon Zero Dawn talk title:** the GDC session list and the Grok pass give "GPU-Based **Run-Time** Procedural Placement in Horizon Zero Dawn"; Guerrilla's own page and slides say "GPU-Based Procedural Placement in Horizon Zero Dawn". Same talk; this file uses Guerrilla's title. [https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/gpu-based-procedural-placement-in-horizon-zero-dawn · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **No Man's Sky and the Superformula:** a 2015 New Yorker passage (quoted by Ars Technica) describes Murray typing the Superformula into a test planet; Murray said in 2016 the game "doesn't actually use" it. Both statements are reported; which is true of the shipped code is not known. [https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2016/07/no-mans-sky-faces-potential-patent-fight-over-use-of-superformula/ · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Far Cry 5 world size:** the slide says "about 100 km square". That can be read as 100 km² or as 100 km on a side; the slide does not say which, and 604,824 entities per km² only fits a memory budget under the first reading (my inference). [https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2018/presentations/ProceduralWorldGeneration.pdf · accessed 2026-11-17]
- **Rain World's design intent:** Jakobsson says enemies designed to "care for itself" become "super difficult and frustrating", while Therrien says restricting where creatures go turned the game into "a basic platformer". The team kept the chaos and added player-side help; the two statements are a trade-off, not a contradiction. [https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/crafting-the-complex-chaotic-ecosystem-of-i-rain-world-i- · accessed 2026-11-17]
