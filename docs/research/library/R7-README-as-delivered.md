## Key corrections and disagreements

1. **U1:** the delivered tarantula "tarsus lengths" are mass cells; the table has no tarsus length.
2. **W50, bicycle self-stability:** the benchmark bicycle is stable between 4.292 and 6.024 m/s; Wikipedia's 5.3 and 8 m/s are a different example bike (values from a search extract of the preprint).
3. **W45, rolling resistance 0.0385-0.0730:** the numbers are Morin's stage coach at a walk on muddy broken-stone road (Baker 1918, Table 5, p. 17), not a dirt road or snow.
4. **W56 and W58, M1A1 Abrams:** maker sheet gives 67.6 tons, 41.5 mph governed and 13.8 psi (95.1 kPa), against the page's 63 tons, 45 mph and 103 kPa.
5. **W57, Leopard 2A6:** maker figures for the A6M are 65 t, 3.77-4.00 m wide, 2.64 m high (page: 62.3 t, 3.75 m, 3.0 m).
6. **W30, Atlas 2016:** maker says about 5 ft 9 in and 180 lb, not 150 cm and 80 kg. **W67, ASW 22:** 850 kg belongs to the self-launching BLE; the 26.58 m BL is 750 kg.
7. **Universal Robots arms (W22, W23, W26):** UR5e 151 mm footprint and 20.7 kg, UR10e 33.3 kg, UR30 63.5 kg on current sheets.
8. **W64 and W66, C-130 and F-16C:** several Wikipedia figures differ from the USAF fact sheets (for example F-16C 37,500 lb max take-off against 42,300).
9. **W14 and W17, *T. rex*:** Hutchinson et al. 2011 give Sue about 9,500 kg (minimal model) against Erickson's 5,650 kg, and peak growth 1,790 kg a year, more than twice Erickson's.
10. **U22:** the 79 m Secchi depth is Gieskes et al. 1987 (Weddell Sea), not Berman et al. 1985. **U16:** the bat digit minima are *Eptesicus furinalis*, not *E. fuscus*.
11. **W82:** the bat paper is free to read but carries no licence; treat as all rights reserved, not CC BY 4.0. **W76:** PET refractive index 1.569 (measured), not 1.575.
12. **Inside the data:** DS1 files cave-bear papers under *Ursus arctos* and mixes Pleistocene wolf tibiae with modern ones (R7-01); a glaucous-winged gull mass of 0.08 kg in DS2's source table is an error (R7-2C); vertebral counts for horse, pig and brown bear look off against usual veterinary values (R7-2A); the milk-snake "0.22 BL/s" is per undulation period, so about 0.11 BL/s (R7-2E).

## Dryad in short
- All three data sets are **free** and **CC0 1.0**.
- File bytes need a human in a browser (a "Confirm you are human" check) or a free Dryad API token; scripts get 401/403.
- **DS1 is done:** the owner supplied the Serio et al. 2024 files (Data_1 to Data_6 and README, in ds-zip/), read in R7-01. DS2 has a GPL-3.0 GitHub mirror of the measured tables; DS3's amplitude envelopes are on Zenodo 4623882 (AFL-3.0), and its per-species wavelength and frequency are now read from the byte-identical Zenodo copy of the Dryad deposit (record 5646507; R7-5).
