# A16 — Muscle attachments as shares along bones

Accessed 2026-11-19.

## Result

**Not found** as numbers in an open source: no open table gives origin/insertion positions as a percentage of bone length (e.g., "deltoid tuberosity at x% of humerus length from the head") for men and women.

## What exists instead (descriptions, not numbers)

- Gray, H. (1918), *Anatomy of the Human Body*, 20th ed., Lea & Febiger (public domain), https://www.bartleby.com/lit-hub/anatomy-of-the-human-body/ — verbal origins and insertions (not re-read this round, so nothing is quoted here).
- Spalteholz, W. (1906), *Hand-Atlas of Human Anatomy*, Vol. 1 (English ed. by L. F. Barker), public domain (Commons tag read: PD-old-70 / PD mark). Bone plates with origins and insertions drawn as coloured, labelled areas. Downloaded this round: Fig 111/112 (scapula), 115/116 (clavicle), 121-122 (humerus), 128-129 (radius/ulna), 150/151 (hand bones), 184-185 (tibia/fibula), 202/203 (foot bones). Files are in images/2-shoulder-arm, 3-hands and 4-feet, named spalteholz-1906-fig*. These are the best source for reading attachment positions along bones. Any share measured from them would be "computed by me", and none was measured this round.
- Dempster 1955 (A12) gives joint-centre and centre-of-gravity proportions along segments, not attachments.
- The Sobotta 1909 and Richer plates in images/ (PD) show attachment areas drawn on bones (e.g., Sobotta humerus/forearm muscle-attachment figures where downloaded), which can be measured from the drawing; any such measurement would be "computed by me" and was **not** done this round, per the brief's "don't trace" rule.
