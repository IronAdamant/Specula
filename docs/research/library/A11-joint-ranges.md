## Sources disagree

- **Ankle dorsiflexion:** MSIS 5th–95th men 8.1–19.9 vs CDC passive mean men 12.7 (20–44). Consistent in scale; MSIS is active astronaut candidates, CDC is passive goniometry.
- **Shoulder flexion:** MSIS 95th percentile values above 200° (men 210.9, women 217.0) exceed the CDC means (~169–172) by far. MSIS likely includes trunk/shoulder-girdle contribution in its method; not resolved.
- **Thumb MCP flexion:** Barakat 2013 mean 60° vs Boike 2025 55–57°; IP flexion 88° vs 71–73°. Different samples (age 26 vs 42.5) and methods.
- **GH : ST ratio:** skin-sensor Nakayama 2018 implies about 2.2–3.0 : 1 for men (2.2–2.6 outside the scapular plane's 30° step) and 3.3–6.1 : 1 for women (computed by me); bone-pin Braman 2009 gives 2.3–2.7 : 1 for raising (sexes pooled). The classic 2 : 1 (Inman 1944) sits at the low end.
- **Finger study ages:** PMC11436331 methods say volunteers 18–60, but Table 1 has an 11–20 age band with 12 people.

## Not found

- **Scapular and clavicular angles at every 30° by plane from bone-pin data** — Ludewig 2009's appendix tables were blocked (PMC served a challenge page); link only: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2657311/
- **Arm-raised steps at 0/45/90/135/180°** exactly: sources sample 30/60/90/120°. Values at 135° and 180° not found.
- **Finger and thumb ROM by sex in a large adult-only sample with open data:** not found. Hume et al. 1990 (*J Hand Surg Am*, young adults, by sex) is behind a paywall; not used.
- **Toe ROM (passive range of each MTP/IP joint) by sex:** not found in an open source; only walking peaks (men only) above.
- **Wrist and ankle ranges by build:** not found.
