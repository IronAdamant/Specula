## Summary

- **Sex:** in adults, men's faces are larger in almost every linear measure, but not uniformly. Relative to
  the ~8% stature difference, the **head box differs least** (head breadth +4%, length +5%, circumference
  +2.4%), **eye spacing differs little** (IPD +4%; intercanthal d = 0.6), and the **lower face, jaw and nose
  differ most** (bigonial +9%, nose breadth +10%, face length +8%, nasal ala length d = 2.0, lower facial
  depth d = 2.1). **Lip vermilion heights do not differ** (d = 0.05). Ears are ~8% larger in men.
- **Age (growth):** head (cranial) size is ~90–94% of the young-adult value by ages 3–6, but face height,
  nose and mouth width are only ~70–80% then and finish growing in the teens (later in boys).
  Most sex differences are small before puberty and jump after 13–15.
- **Age (adult ageing, women, Korea):** from 20–39 to 60–79 the eye opening narrows and shortens (fissure
  height −28%), the upper lid space grows, the nose widens (+11%), the philtrum/upper lip lengthens (+20%),
  the lower red lip thins (−13%) and the mouth widens, while total face height is unchanged.

## Sources disagree

- **fWHR:** the original skull paper that Kramer re-examines (not read here) is the usual basis for claiming male > female fWHR;
  Kramer's 2017 meta-analysis finds the effect small in skulls and absent in White faces. Treat as contested.
- **Face width growth:** 3DFN puts girls' maximum facial width at 100% of the 22–25 value by 13–15 while boys
  reach ~96% then and ~99–101% by 16–21 — consistent with later male growth; but the 3DFN group sizes for
  13–18 are small (43–56 per sex), so year-by-year timing is uncertain.
- **Lip height with age:** 3DFN young adults (US, both sexes) show lower vermilion height falling from 19–21
  to 22–25 in both sexes (M 9.6→9.0; F 9.5→8.9), while the Korean study shows the main thinning after 40.
  Different populations and methods; both point the same direction.
- **Intercanthal width and age:** Kwon reports a U-shaped pattern (36.3 → 34.0 → 35.7 mm), unlike the
  monotonic fissure narrowing; likely sampling noise or landmark drift with lid laxity.
- **Absolute nose width:** Korean women 20–39 have al–al 39.6 mm vs 3DFN US white women 22–25 at 32.4 mm —
  a real population difference (Farkas 2005 abstract also reports wider noses in Asian groups vs NAW).
